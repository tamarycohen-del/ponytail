# Stage 1: Plot Outline (for approval)

**Title:** IN THIS TOWN, EVERYONE HAS AN OPPOSITE CLONE…
**Runtime target:** ~10:00 (about 1,450–1,550 narrated words at ~150 wpm)
**Narration:** third person, one storyteller, present-day casual American English.

## Logline
In a Roblox town where every kid gets a clone that does the opposite of what they do, shy Alex's clone seems to hate him. Then he tells it, "I hope you NEVER become famous," and wakes up to find it's the most famous person in town. His clone was never doing the opposite of his actions. It was doing the opposite of his wishes.

## World rules (fixed so the story stays logical)
1. **Clone Day:** when a kid turns 12, they step into the light of the **Mirror Fountain** in the town square. The light flashes white and their opposite clone steps out.
2. **Normal clones flip actions:** smile → angry, walk → run, sit → stand, wave → turn away, sleep → wide awake.
3. **The exception (planted, explained later):** if you make a wish *while standing in the light*, the light flashes **purple**. That clone flips your **wishes**, not your actions.
4. **Only the fountain can undo it:** step back into the light with a clear mind (no wish) and the light flashes white again.
5. A wish only counts if it's about the clone. (So Alex can't use it to control the whole world.)

## Story beats with timestamps

**0:00–0:15 HOOK**
Cold open on the town square at sunrise. A huge crowd surrounds Alex's clone as it signs autographs. Alex hides behind a tree.
> "In this town, everyone has a clone that does the exact opposite of everything they do. And this morning, Alex's clone became the most famous person in town… because Alex told it not to."

**0:15–1:45 ACT 1: THE RULE**
- What Clone Day is, plus quick demos of the flip: smile/angry, walk/run, sit/stand, wave/turn away.
- At first the whole town thinks it's hilarious.
- Alex's own Clone Day. He's terrified. Standing in the light, he squeezes his eyes shut and thinks, *"Please, I hope this goes okay."* The light flickers **purple** for half a second. Nobody notices except Old Gary, who frowns. (Plant.)
- His clone steps out. It looks exactly like him.

**1:45–3:45 ACT 2: HOW THE TOWN USES CLONES (funny middle)**
- **Ty**, the laziest kid alive, lies on his couch all day. His clone works three jobs. Ty becomes the richest kid in town.
- **The Backwards Race:** owners walk as slowly as possible so their clones sprint. Slowest walker wins. Ty wins without getting up.
- **Mia** goes to sleep and her clone cleans her room all night. When Mia cries at a sad movie, her clone laughs so hard it falls off the couch.
- Grumpy pizza chef, cheerful clone waiter: best reviews in town.
- The narrator turns: "Everyone's clone was useful. Everyone's except Alex's."

**3:45–5:15 ACT 3: THE CLONE THAT HATES ALEX**
- Alex's clone breaks the rules. When Alex sits, it sometimes sits too. It seems to do whatever hurts him most.
- Alex **hopes** it'll help at his pizza-delivery job. It eats every pizza.
- He **hopes** it won't embarrass him at school. It dances on the cafeteria table.
- He **hopes** Mia will like it. It turns its back on her every time she waves.
- He **hopes** it'll be nice to him for once. It pushes him into the pool.
- Night. In his bedroom, soaked and angry, Alex shouts the key line: **"I hope you NEVER become famous!"** The clone, standing in the corner, slowly smiles.

**5:15–7:15 ACT 4: FAMOUS (escalation + explanation)**
- Back to the opening scene. Overnight the clone finished the impossible Tower Obby, rescued a kitten from the clock tower, and fixed the broken fountain lights. Mayor Buckley hangs a gold medal on it.
- **The mystery:** it no longer acts like an opposite clone. When people wave, it waves back. When they smile, it smiles. The town loves it: "The only *nice* clone in town!"
- Alex runs to Old Gary. Gary tells him about the purple light: *"You made a wish in the light, kid. Your clone isn't the opposite of what you DO. It's the opposite of what you WANT."*
- **Test scene:** Alex stares at the clone and thinks, *"I hope you don't jump."* The clone jumps. *"I hope you don't sit down."* It sits. Confirmed.
- Every "mean" moment replays in fast flashbacks, reframed: it never hated him. It flipped every hope he had.
- **Escalation:** Alex panics: *"I hope it doesn't take my place."* Too late, he just wished it. Next scenes: the clone sits in Alex's chair at dinner, Mom serves it pancakes, and Mia invites the clone to the movies. Every worried hope makes it worse, and Alex can't stop worrying.

**7:15–9:00 ACT 5: THE FOUNTAIN (resolution)**
- Gary: the only way to undo it is to step back into the light **with no wish at all**. But the mayor is holding a ceremony at the fountain tonight, in front of the whole town.
- Shy Alex walks onto the stage anyway. He tells the truth: "It's famous because I wished it wouldn't be." The crowd goes quiet.
- He steps into the light, eyes open, mind clear. The light flashes **white**. The clone blinks. Alex smiles, and the clone frowns. Alex waves, and the clone turns away. It's a normal opposite clone again.
- Alex laughs. The crowd cheers, this time for *Alex*, the kid who was brave enough to tell the truth. Mia sits next to him on the fountain's edge.

**9:00–9:45 FINAL TWIST**
- Alex steps out of the fountain, relieved, but one foot is still in the light. He thinks, *"I really hope this is finally over."*
- Tiny purple flicker. Nobody notices this time, not even Gary.
- Last shot: behind Alex, the clone (frowning, like a normal clone) stops, turns its head straight to the camera, and slowly smiles.
> "It was not over."

**9:45–10:00 OUTRO / COMMENT HOOK**
> "So… if YOUR clone did the opposite of everything you wished for, what would you wish for? Tell me in the comments. And what do you think Alex's clone will do next?"

## Why the twist is logical (checklist)
- Purple light planted at 1:15, explained at ~6:00, reused at 9:15. ✅
- Every "hate" moment is a flipped hope, and all four get reframed in the flashback. ✅
- "NEVER become famous" → famous, fully explained by the rule. ✅
- The clone acting "nice" (waving back, smiling) is explained the same way. Watching the crowd adore it, Alex keeps thinking *"I hope everyone sees how awful you really are"*, and it flips into the nicest clone in town. Only Alex's wishes count. (Narrated in Act 4.) ✅
- The ending pays off the rule a third time without needing new lore. ✅

## Creative decisions made (documented, not blocking)
- Clone Day at age 12 explains why only kids get clones without making it a whole-town origin story.
- Narrator is third person (not Alex), so there's no character dialogue. Alex's thoughts are narrated in quotes, never voiced by a second actor.
- No mirror-image costume details (e.g. a watch on the opposite wrist). AI generators can't place them reliably and they'd break the "looks exactly like Alex" rule.
- Ty, Mayor Buckley, and Old Gary are new characters, added to carry the comedy, the escalation, and the explanation.
